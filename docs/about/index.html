The person behind the stories

Hello, I’m Anoop.

I’m Anoop Tej Thotapalli, a father and an engineer. Wonderabouts brings together things I care about: stories, curiosity, family time, and learning through everyday experiences.

My work involves DevOps, platforms and AI. But this is not a technology classroom. You do not need to know an engineering term to enjoy a story or try an activity here.

Meet Wobble and his world

Wobble is our main character: a curious orange scout robot with blue sensor eyes and rubber treads. He explores alongside children who bring their own questions and ideas. He does not know everything, and learning goes both ways.

The first published adventure follows Wobble on a delivery that does not go to plan. The website’s illustrations introduce the wider world of children learning with him. These are fictional characters, not portraits of my family.

Stories first. Then something to try.

Wonderabouts brings together stories, activities, and everyday discoveries during intentional, time-bounded sessions. Wobble is a learning buddy for limited gadget time filled with knowledge, new lessons, and discoveries. Families choose a session together, with room for hands-on play and breaks. Over time, I want to explore robotics-inspired adventures, digital parenting, food, outings and family life. You can watch Wobble’s captioned welcome, then check in together and choose your adventure.

The invitation is simple: read something, try something if you like, and talk together. There is no requirement to finish an activity or find a particular answer.

How I approach the content

  • Experience stays honest. I will distinguish personal experiences from fictional stories and proposed activities.
  • Research stays traceable. Research-led articles will link to their sources and separate demonstrated findings from fictional ideas.
  • No promised transformations. Activities are invitations, not clinical advice or tested interventions.
  • Feelings have room. A child does not need to become cheerful, productive or successful to deserve support.
  • Gadget time has a purpose. Explore knowledge and new lessons in a session chosen with a grown-up, then make room for play, rest and life beyond the screen.
  • Family privacy matters. Sensitive family details and unpublished writing do not belong in a public content pipeline.

Who is this for?

Children and the adults around them, with room for pre-teens and teens too. Each activity suggests ways to adapt it. Age labels are guides, not assessments of ability. Younger children should explore with a grown-up.

Family sessions

Start with five picture questions about your day. A grown-up reviews anything unfinished, not applicable, or needing help, then confirms the time you choose together. There is no universal time limit. School reflections are conversations, not tests.

The timer begins when you open your adventure. It continues if you change tabs or close the browser until the chosen end time; use Pause & save for an intentional break. A gentle reminder appears before the pause screen. A grown-up can approve more time or you can stop and return another day.

Reading sections and activity steps are saved in this browser. New sessions begin with a fresh check-in. This simple confirmation is a family routine, not verified adult identity or a parental-control lock. Public pages and browser data cannot enforce supervision.

We never listen to children or ask for evidence. Check-in answers are held only while you complete the current check-in, then cleared. Read about saving and privacy.

About the artwork

The launch illustrations were created with AI image generation. Wobble’s adventures are fiction. The artwork is not evidence that an activity has been tested with a family.
