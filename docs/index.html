Read Wobble’s welcome

Hi! I’m Wobble. Welcome to Wonderabouts with Anoop!

Here, we explore stories, try activities, and ask big questions together.

Before today’s adventure, let’s check in about your day.

Was today a school day? How did it go?

Have you had a chance to freshen up?

Is your homework done, or do you need some help?

Have you had time to play or move today, outside if it was safe?

And tell me about the chores you did to help at home. Maybe you put away your toys, helped set the table, watered a plant, or did something else!

Choose the picture that fits your day. You can say ‘Done,’ ‘Not yet,’ ‘Not today,’ or ‘I need help.’ Every day is different, and it’s okay to ask for help!

With your grown-up, check what needs doing and choose how long we’ll explore.

Then we can start today’s story and activity!

Before our time is up, we’ll get a gentle reminder. When we stop, we’ll save our place for another day.

Ready? Let’s see where our curiosity takes us!

A balanced day, a little wonder

The adventure doesn’t end here.

I’m Anoop, a father and an engineer. Wonderabouts is where stories turn into things we can try, questions we can ask, and moments we can share.

There is room for stories on a screen and discoveries away from it. Choose a session together, take breaks, and come back another day. Muddy shoes, cardboard boxes, and new flavors are welcome too.

A little about Anoop & Wonderabouts →

Different ages. Different ways to join.

Little explorers can read and play with a grown-up. Older children can take on extra challenges. Start with what feels right for your child, and leave room for breaks.
