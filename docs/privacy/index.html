Wonderabouts is a reading and activity website by Anoop Tej Thotapalli. You can use the launch version without creating an account or sending check-in answers to us.

What this site does not collect

We do not provide accounts, comment forms, newsletter forms, uploads or payments. We do not ask children for names, ages, photographs, email addresses or other personal information.

No advertising scripts, third-party analytics scripts, or tracking cookies are added by this website. Fonts, images and Wobble’s video are served with the site, not fetched from social-media embeds. The welcome uses a synthetic character voice, native playback controls and captions, with no autoplay or recording.

What stays in your browser

The family session uses browser local storage to save the chosen duration, session status and end time, and your last story section or activity step. One shared session is used per browser, without child profiles. We do not send this information to a server. Anyone using the same browser can see or change it.

Check-in answers and grown-up exception choices are held in page memory while you complete the check-in. They are cleared when you start, restart, refresh, or leave that page. We do not keep a history of chores, school days, or answers.

Saved progress remains until you clear it here or clear your browser data. It does not sync to another device. Private browsing may discard it. If persistent storage is blocked, we try temporary tab storage and explain that it ends when the tab closes. If both are blocked, timed sessions cannot start.

Approval applies to the current session on the browser’s local calendar day. Returning on a new day requires another check-in. This is a family agreement, not identity verification.

This will end the session and remove your saved place in this browser. It will not affect other devices.

Hosting and external services

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to operate their services. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Links to other websites take you to services with their own policies. There are no embedded YouTube videos or social feeds in this launch version.

Search visibility

The site is intended to be publicly discoverable. Google Search Console may be used to understand how pages appear in Google Search. No Google Analytics or advertising tag is installed.

For families

Explore with a grown-up when needed. Please do not share private information about children in public posts when discussing the activities. Fictional story characters and generated illustrations are not depictions of identifiable children.

Future changes

If we add features that collect information, this page will be updated to describe them before they launch.
